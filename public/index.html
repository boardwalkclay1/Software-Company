The Guild is a trading education app built inside a creative medieval storyline.

It teaches options trading, discipline, strategy, and long‑term thinking through a world of guilds, ranks, scrolls, and ancient knowledge.

The Guild is also connected to the Comic Universe — characters, lore, and events overlap between the app and the comics.

Inside the app, users can:

• Learn options trading
• Follow structured lessons
• Unlock ranks and achievements
• Explore lore tied to the Comic Universe
• Access the Comic Universe repo

It’s a real educational system wrapped in a cinematic world.
