AUTOMATION CATALOG – FULL DESCRIPTIONS

--------------------------------------------------
1. LEAD CAPTURE → CRM SYNC
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Lead Capture → CRM Sync

SHORT SUMMARY:
Automatically takes new leads from forms or lead sources and sends them into a CRM or database, fully organized, with no manual copy-paste.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation “listens” for new leads from one or more sources, such as:
  - Website contact forms
  - Typeform / Jotform / Google Forms
  - Facebook Lead Ads or other ad platforms
- Every time someone submits their information, the automation:
  - Collects their name, email, phone number, and any other fields (budget, service type, message, etc.)
  - Checks if this person already exists in the CRM (to avoid duplicates)
  - If they are new, it creates a new contact record
  - If they already exist, it updates their existing record instead of creating a duplicate
- It can also:
  - Add tags (for example: “Facebook Ad Lead”, “Website Lead”, “High Budget”)
  - Add the lead to a specific pipeline or stage (for example: “New Lead”)
  - Send an email or SMS notification to the business owner or sales team
  - Log the lead in a backup spreadsheet (for reporting and safety)

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- No more manually copying leads from email into a CRM or spreadsheet.
- No more losing leads because someone forgot to log them.
- Every lead is stored in one place, organized and ready for follow-up.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Any business that collects leads online and wants them automatically organized in a CRM.

2. SOCIAL MEDIA AUTO-POSTER
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Social Media Auto-Poster

SHORT SUMMARY:
Automatically posts content to social media accounts on a schedule. The user only has to prepare content and drop it into a folder or form.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation is designed to remove the need to manually log into social media and post.
- The user prepares content in advance:
  - Images, videos, or text posts
  - Captions, hashtags, and links
- The automation watches a specific source, such as:
  - A folder (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox)
  - A spreadsheet with post text and image links
  - A form where the user submits content
- On a schedule (for example, every day at 10 AM), the automation:
  - Picks the next piece of content
  - Uploads the image or video to the chosen platform(s)
  - Adds the caption, hashtags, and mentions
  - Publishes immediately or schedules it for a specific time
- It can also:
  - Log each published post in a spreadsheet (date, time, platform, link)
  - Send a confirmation email or message saying “Your post has been published”

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- The user doesn’t have to remember to post.
- No need to log into multiple platforms.
- Consistent posting without daily effort.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Creators, small businesses, agencies, or anyone who wants consistent social media activity with minimal effort.

3. AUTO-INVOICE GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Auto-Invoice Generator

SHORT SUMMARY:
Automatically creates and sends professional invoices when a job is completed, a form is submitted, or a payment event occurs.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation is triggered when:
  - A client submits a “work completed” form
  - A project is marked as done in a project management tool
  - A sale or order is recorded in an e-commerce system
- When triggered, it:
  - Uses a pre-designed invoice template with the business logo and details
  - Fills in:
    - Client name and contact info
    - Description of the service or product
    - Quantity, price, taxes, and total amount
    - Invoice number and date
  - Generates a PDF invoice file
- Then it:
  - Emails the invoice PDF to the client
  - Optionally CCs the business owner or accountant
  - Saves a copy of the invoice in a folder (e.g., “Invoices/2026”)
  - Logs the invoice in a spreadsheet or accounting sheet (date, client, amount, status)

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- No more manually creating invoices in Word or Excel.
- No forgetting to send invoices.
- All invoices are stored and tracked automatically.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Freelancers, agencies, service providers, and small businesses that invoice clients regularly.

4. EMAIL DRIP SEQUENCE ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Email Drip Sequence Engine

SHORT SUMMARY:
Sends a series of pre-written emails to new leads or customers over time, automatically, based on a schedule.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation starts when:
  - A new lead is added to a list
  - Someone fills out a form
  - Someone makes a purchase or signs up
- Once triggered, it:
  - Sends a first “Welcome” email immediately or shortly after
  - Schedules follow-up emails (for example):
    - Day 1: Welcome + introduction
    - Day 3: Story or case study
    - Day 5: Value email (tips, guide, or tutorial)
    - Day 7: Offer or call-to-action
- Each email can:
  - Use the person’s name and other details (personalization)
  - Include links, buttons, and calls-to-action
- The automation can:
  - Stop the sequence if the person replies
  - Move the person to another list or segment after the sequence ends
  - Track opens and clicks (if supported by the email platform)

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- Ensures every new lead gets consistent follow-up.
- Saves time writing and sending individual emails.
- Warms up leads automatically before a sales conversation.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Anyone who wants automatic follow-up: coaches, agencies, course creators, SaaS, etc.

5. CLIENT ONBOARDING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Client Onboarding Pipeline

SHORT SUMMARY:
Automatically handles the steps that happen after a new client signs up, giving them a smooth, professional onboarding experience.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation starts when:
  - A client pays
  - A contract is signed
  - A “New Client” form is submitted
- Once triggered, it:
  - Sends a welcome email with:
    - A thank-you message
    - Next steps
    - Important links (portal, calendar, resources)
  - Creates a client folder in cloud storage (e.g., “Clients/Client Name”)
  - Adds the client to the CRM with the correct tags and pipeline stage
  - Creates tasks for the internal team (e.g., “Schedule kickoff call”, “Prepare documents”)
  - Optionally sends the client:
    - An onboarding questionnaire
    - A link to book a call
- It can also:
  - Notify the business owner or team in Slack/Email
  - Log the onboarding event in a spreadsheet

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- No more messy, inconsistent onboarding.
- Every client gets the same professional experience.
- The team knows exactly what to do next.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Service businesses, agencies, consultants, and anyone who works with clients.

6. AUTO-CONTRACT SENDER
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Auto-Contract Sender

SHORT SUMMARY:
Automatically generates and sends contracts for e-signature when a client reaches a certain stage (e.g., “Ready to start”).

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation starts when:
  - A client fills out a “Ready to start” form
  - A deal in the CRM moves to a specific stage (e.g., “Contract Needed”)
- Once triggered, it:
  - Uses a contract template (with your legal text)
  - Fills in:
    - Client name and business name
    - Project or service description
    - Price, payment terms, and dates
  - Creates a contract document (PDF or e-sign document)
- Then it:
  - Sends the contract to the client via an e-sign platform (e.g., DocuSign, HelloSign, etc.)
  - Tracks whether the client has opened and signed it
  - Sends reminders if it’s not signed after a certain number of days
  - Saves the signed contract in a client folder
  - Notifies the business owner when it’s signed

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- No more manually editing contracts for each client.
- No forgetting to send contracts or follow up.
- All signed contracts are stored and easy to find.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Any business that uses contracts or agreements with clients.

7. CONTENT REPURPOSING ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Content Repurposing Engine

SHORT SUMMARY:
Takes long-form content (video, audio, or text) and turns it into multiple smaller pieces of content for social media, blogs, and emails.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation starts when:
  - A long video, audio file, or document is uploaded to a specific folder
  - Or a link to a video (e.g., YouTube) is submitted
- Once triggered, it:
  - Extracts the audio or text (using transcription if needed)
  - Identifies key moments, quotes, or sections
  - Creates:
    - Short video clips (for Reels, Shorts, TikTok)
    - Social media captions
    - Tweet / post threads
    - Short summaries
    - Bullet-point highlights
- It then:
  - Saves all outputs in organized folders (e.g., “Clips”, “Captions”, “Summaries”)
  - Optionally sends a summary email with links to all generated content

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- Turns one piece of content into many without manual editing.
- Saves hours of work per week.
- Helps clients stay active on multiple platforms using content they already have.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Content creators, coaches, agencies, and anyone who makes long-form content.

8. APPOINTMENT SCHEDULER + REMINDERS
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
Appointment Scheduler + Reminders

SHORT SUMMARY:
Lets customers book appointments and automatically sends confirmations and reminders to reduce no-shows.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation connects a booking form or scheduling tool to a calendar and messaging system.
- When a customer books an appointment:
  - The appointment is added to the calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.)
  - The customer receives a confirmation email or SMS with:
    - Date and time
    - Location or meeting link
    - Any instructions
- Before the appointment:
  - The automation sends reminder messages (for example):
    - 24 hours before
    - 1 hour before
- It can also:
  - Update the CRM with the appointment details
  - Notify the business owner or team
  - Log all appointments in a spreadsheet

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- Fewer missed appointments.
- No manual reminder messages.
- Clear, automatic communication with customers.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Any business that books calls, meetings, or sessions.

9. E-COMMERCE ORDER PROCESSOR
--------------------------------------------------

NAME:
E-commerce Order Processor

SHORT SUMMARY:
Automatically processes new orders from an online store and keeps everything organized and up to date.

WHAT IT DOES (PLAIN LANGUAGE):
- This automation starts when:
  - A new order is placed in an online store (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.)
- Once triggered, it:
  - Captures order details:
    - Customer name and contact info
    - Products purchased
    - Prices, taxes, and total
    - Shipping address
  - Sends an order confirmation email (if not already sent by the store)
  - Updates inventory levels (if connected)
  - Logs the order in a spreadsheet or database
  - Adds the customer to the CRM with tags like “Customer”, “Product X Buyer”
- It can also:
  - Trigger a shipping workflow
  - Send internal notifications to the fulfillment team
  - Start a post-purchase email sequence

WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES:
- Keeps orders organized without manual data entry.
- Makes sure inventory, CRM, and records are always up to date.
- Saves time and reduces mistakes.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
- Online stores, product businesses, and anyone selling physical or digital products.
