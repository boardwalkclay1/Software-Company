Invent Something

This page is for people who feel like they have ideas but don’t know where to start. You don’t need a patent, a drawing, or a business plan. You just need a problem and a vision.

Step 1: Pick a Problem Area

Home & Daily Life

Things that annoy you at home, in the kitchen, with kids, with chores.

Work & Business

Slow processes, repeated tasks, confusing tools, missing systems.

Health & Fitness

Tracking, reminders, routines, accountability, data, habits.

Hobbies & Fun

Games, tools, organizers, trackers, communities.

Step 2: Describe the Frustration

Finish this sentence in your head: “I hate that every time I…” That sentence is the seed of an invention.

Step 3: Imagine the Shortcut

Now finish this one: “I wish there was a way to…” That’s your product.

Step 4: Send It to Me

You don’t have to know if it should be an app, a website, a device, or a mix. Tell me the problem and the dream version of the solution — I’ll design the system.
