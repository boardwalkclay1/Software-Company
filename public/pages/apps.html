Real Apps

These are real, production apps you can use right now. Each one runs in the browser and can be installed to your home screen like a native app.

The Guild

A cinematic family-based knowledge system with trading information right on the home screen.

The Guild is built around the idea of a family-led knowledge house, where the Head of the Guild teaches the laws, rituals, and wisdom of the Dragon Arena. When you open the app, you land directly on live trading information and guidance — not buried in menus, but right on the home screen.

It’s designed for people who want a structured, story-driven way to learn about markets, discipline, and long-term thinking, wrapped in a cinematic guild experience.

How to Add The Guild to Your Home Screen

  • Open: the-guild-855.pages.dev in your mobile browser.
  • Tap the Share icon (iOS Safari) or the ⋮ menu (Android Chrome).
  • Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  • The Guild installs as a full-screen app with trading info on the home screen.
