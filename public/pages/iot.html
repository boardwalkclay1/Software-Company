Security, Safety & Awareness

IoT sensors can protect your home, your business, and even your vehicle. With the right setup, you can detect motion, presence, vibration, heat, sound, or even a laser trip line — all feeding into a real‑time dashboard.

Examples of What You Can Do

  • Laser Trip Sensors — invisible beams that trigger alerts when crossed.
  • Human Presence Sensors — detect body heat or micro‑movement.
  • Motion Sensors — perfect for lights, alarms, or silent notifications.
  • Vibration Sensors — detect tampering, break‑ins, or movement.
  • Sound Sensors — detect glass breaking, loud noises, or engine starts.
  • Door/Window Sensors — instant alerts when opened.
  • Car Security Sensors — detect presence around your vehicle.
  • Motor/Relay Control — activate fans, pumps, motors, or lights remotely.
