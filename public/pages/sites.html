What Is a Website Template?

A website template is a fully designed, working layout that shows how your site can look and behave — with real sections, real flows, and real interactions. These are not generic theme‑store templates.

Every template on this page was built by Go Time Software as a live example for clients: you can scroll, click, and experience the structure before we customize it to your brand, content, and goals.

How Your Custom Site Is Built

  • Pick a starting point: choose one or more templates that feel close to your vision.
  • We customize everything: colors, fonts, layout, sections, copy, and integrations.
  • We wire it to real systems: forms, dashboards, automations, payments, or IoT if needed.
  • We ship a production‑ready site: fast, cinematic, and built specifically for your business.
